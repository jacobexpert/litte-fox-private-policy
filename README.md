# Privacy Policy for Tee the Fox: Cozy Puzzle Adventures

**Effective Date:** October 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 20, 2026  

Welcome to **Tee the Fox: Cozy Puzzle Adventures** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy of our players, especially young children and families. 

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information in full compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (**COPPA**), the General Data Protection Regulation (**GDPR**), and Google Play’s **Families Policy**.

---

## 1. No Personal Data Collection
**Tee the Fox** is designed as a completely offline, safe, and family-friendly puzzle game. 

* **We DO NOT collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII)** such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device identifiers.
* **We DO NOT require any user registration or account creation.**

---

## 2. No Ads and No Social Features
To ensure a 100% safe environment for children and a distraction-free experience for families:

* **No Advertisements:** Our game contains zero third-party ads.
* **No Social Chat / Communication:** There are no chat rooms, messaging features, or social media integrations within the game.
* **No In-App Tracking:** We do not track user activity across other apps or websites.

---

## 3. Data Storage on Device
All game progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device using standard local storage (Web Storage / Native Storage). This data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to any external servers or third parties.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services
Our application does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, or advertising networks that collect data from users or children.

---

## 5. Compliance with Children’s Privacy Laws (COPPA & GDPR-K)
Our app fully complies with COPPA and GDPR-K regulations. Because we do not collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions), parents can feel completely confident letting their children play **Tee the Fox**.

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to maintain compliance with relevant laws and store policies. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
